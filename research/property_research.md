## ADU rules that apply (OC Zoning Code Sec. 7-9-90)
- One detached ADU allowed by **ministerial permit** (no public hearing).
- A new ADU located partly/fully in the R1 side or rear setback is limited to **800 sq ft, 16 ft height, min 4 ft from side & rear property lines.** An 800 sq ft unit in this back yard falls in this category.
- Max 1,200 sq ft only if the whole ADU stays outside the normal R1 setbacks (front 20 ft, side 5 ft, rear 25 ft). Not practical on this lot.
- Must have direct exterior access; long-term rental OK (>30 days), **no short-term rental**; cannot be sold separately.
- **Parking:** 1 space, may be tandem on existing driveway, **unless** within ½-mile walk of public transit (then none). Show on plans.
- Not counted as new residential use for water/sewer connection fees.
- County checklist: **fire sprinklers required if the ADU is within 5 ft of a property line** or the main house has sprinklers (deferred submittal allowed).
- Apply for a new ADU address after building permit submittal (myOCeServices → Survey Service Request → Addressing).

## Plan check submittal required by OC (Building Plan Check checklist)
PDF scaled drawings: site plan (distances to property lines) · foundation plan & details · floor plan · roof plan ·
exterior elevations · sections · structural plans + **engineer's calculations (stamped)** · Title 24 energy compliance forms ·
**soils report (< 6 months old)** · erosion & sediment control plan · WQMP/BMP exhibit (unless exempt). Incomplete submittals are not accepted.

Planning items on the site plan: north arrow, scale, legend, address, APN, gross/net lot area, zoning, property lines (dimensioned),
setbacks, street centerline, easements, existing/proposed topography (≤2 ft contours), average lot slope, primary house footprint,
all accessory structures, parking, lot coverage, wall/fence heights, new & replaced impervious area, utility run lengths
(water/gas/electric to ADU), sewer, solar (per Title 24).

## ⭐ Option: County Pre-Approved ADU plans
